Lights Off Stories

Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Summary

Lights Off Stories is a personal tool used by its owner to publish original story videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information about anyone else, and it has no public users.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok issues an access token that lets the app upload videos to that account and read the account's basic profile (display name and open ID). That token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used for nothing except uploading the owner's videos.

What the app does not do

It does not read messages, followers, comments, or other people's profiles. It does not track viewers of the videos. It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising services. No information is transferred to third parties except to TikTok itself, in order to deliver the videos.

Data retention

The access token stays on the owner's computer until the owner disconnects the app or deletes the token file. Generated videos and their captions are kept locally by the owner.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), which immediately stops the app from uploading.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above shows the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact details on the Lights Off Stories TikTok profile.
